Somewhere Else

Tags: General

“I hope someone else tries it”, is something I think regularly with some of the less orthodox ideas suggested for government policy. Despite being interesting, they mostly they never happen anywhere but if they do I would prefer the first attempt to not be where I’m living. These are the kind of things that are unlikely to work due to unforeseen consequences, at least on first attempt. However, if they did work, could be hugely beneficial. For example, I hope someone tries a Universal Basic Income at the national level. However due to a large amount of cynicism, I would prefer Australia is not first.

Anyway, a social media ban for kids, I hope someone else tries it. Oh well.
