Link Blog 3

Tags: Links

  • I can waste hours looking at things like this Interactive World History Atlas since 3000 BC.
  • The BBC’s In Our Time is one of the best podcasts available. Although with around 30 years of old episodes it can be hard to find the one you are interested in (especially through RSS). Fear not, the Explore the In Our Time archive has you covered.
  • Why Is Venus Hell and Earth an Eden?. As the title suggests.
  • Learn X in Y minutes is a handy reminder tool for the syntax and basic control of most languages. I’ve already found it useful when needing to code in a long forgotten (by me) language.
  • Send this article to your friend who still thinks the cloud is a good idea. Interesting calculation of cloud vs hosted costs of an online service. I’ve always done the later.
  • BALL x PIT: My game of the year 2025 is a mildly interesting article about what the writer thinks BALL x PIT does right. I haven’t played the game, so can’t comment on that. What I found super interesting was a small point made near the end - the “Easy, Hard, Easy” loops in popular games on Steam. Damn, when I read that, it was a revelation. I have felt it as a player but not conceptualised it as a developer. Definitely something I will look to include in my games.
  • Internet security and avoiding ads (know that the two are intrinsically linked!) is important. How I block all online ads & An easy-to-use digital safety checklist are good lists of things that can be done to help. I haven’t done all the things listed, but certainly have gone through more than the basics.
